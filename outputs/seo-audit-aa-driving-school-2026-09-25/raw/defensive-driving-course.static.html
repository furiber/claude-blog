AA Defensive Driving Course

Completing the NZTA approved AA Defensive Driving Course will help you become a safer driver, better identify hazards and increase your chances of passing your driving test. 

access_time

Eligible drivers who complete the course can reach a full licence up to 6 months sooner*

*Reduction only applies to those eligible to apply for their full licence prior to 25 January 2027; drivers aged 25+ may receive a reduction from six to three months. See the NZTA website or contact us for details.
Book a course

A safer driver in 9 hours

The AA Defensive Driving Course is an NZTA approved Advanced Driving Course aimed at educating drivers how to be safer on the roads.

What does the course include?

Four classroom sessions, an online hazard perception component and a one-hour in-car assessment. The following four steps must be completed in order.

Four classroom sessions

Four two-hour sessions (8 hours in total).

 

  • Interactive group theory using real driving situations filmed on NZ roads. Covers risks, observation and hazard identification. The four sessions must be completed in order.
  • Need to bring a support person or reader/writer assistance? Contact us before the course date.

Online hazard perception

Practice hazard identification and visual search online. 

 

  • Account valid for 28 days.
  • Unlimited attempts.
  • Minimum score: 80% - certificate must be shown to the tutor before the in-car assessment.

In-car session (1 hour)

Individual driving assessment in your own vehicle. 
 
  • The instructor will assess understanding and demonstration of the theory learnt in the classroom sessions.
  • Must be completed within 60 days of your last classroom session.

Get your certificate

Issued after:

 

  • Completing coursework. 
  • Achieving at least 80% in online hazard perception.
  • Passing the in-car session.

If you're eligible to reduce the time on your restricted licence* take your certificate when you apply for your full licence. 

*Reduction only applies to those eligible to apply for their full licence prior to 25 January 2027; drivers aged 25+ may receive a reduction from six to three months. See the NZTA website or contact us for details.
Book a course

Cost & booking

  • From $245 per person.
  • All courses are booked and paid for online.
  • The in-car session is arranged directly with your tutor.

Refunds for cancellations will only be made if we receive notification of cancellation at least 14 days prior to commencement of your original course date. For full details view Defensive Driving Course terms and conditions.

Defensive Driving Course vouchers

You can also buy vouchers for the Defensive Driving Course which you can use at a later date or gift to someone else. The voucher is not a booking but can be redeemed when making a booking.

Vouchers are for courses provided by the AA and cannot be used for other licensed course providers.

When should you take it?

We recommend completing this course when you are on your restricted licence, as it focuses on more experienced driving skills.

menu_book

On your learner licence

Experienced learner licence holders who are confident drivers can attend a Defensive Driving Course. 

directions_car

On your restricted licence - best time to attend

Build a strong foundation as an independent driver and your restricted period may be cut by up to 6 months (drivers aged 25+ may cut their restricted period from six to three months).*

 

stars

On your full licence

You can still benefit from taking the course to refresh your skills, or you may need to take it if you're a police recruit, have a court referral, or are completing a Police Diversion Scheme.

Frequently asked questions

The AA Defensive Driving Course is an NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi-approved advanced driving course available across New Zealand. It combines interactive learning based on real driving situations on New Zealand roads with online hazard training and an individual in-car assessment. The course is designed to help drivers identify hazards, reduce crash risk, improve safe-driving skills and prepare for the next stage of licensing.

The AA Defensive Driving Course can be worthwhile if you want to become a safer, more confident driver, improve hazard awareness or prepare for a practical driving test. It provides structured theory, online practice and a one-hour in-car assessment with practical feedback. Eligible restricted drivers may also be able to apply for their full licence up to six months earlier under the current Graduated Driver Licensing System; check the latest NZTA eligibility rules to confirm.

It is generally best to take the AA Defensive Driving Course after you have obtained your restricted licence, because the course helps build a strong foundation as you begin driving more independently. Experienced learner licence holders who are confident drivers can also attend and must be able to complete the in-car assessment in their own vehicle. Full licence holders can take the course to refresh their skills, and it may also suit police recruits or people completing a court referral or Police Diversion programme.

The AA Defensive Driving Course teaches you to assess and identify hazards, improve visual-search techniques, understand driving risks and road conditions, and apply safer driving practices. The nine-hour course includes four two-hour classroom sessions completed in order, online eDrive learning using real New Zealand road scenarios, and a one-hour individual in-car assessment. The eDrive component requires a score of at least 80%, and the in-car assessment must be successfully completed within 60 days of the final classroom session.

Book an AA Defensive Driving Course online through the AA Defensive Driving Course page by selecting ‘Book a course’, choosing an available location and course, and paying online. Courses are available at locations across New Zealand. Your course tutor will arrange the one-hour in-car assessment during the classroom sessions; if you book with a licensed third-party provider, that provider will contact you directly with course dates and other details. A course voucher can also be bought and redeemed when making a booking, but the voucher itself is not a booking.

AA Driving School is operated by AA Driver Training Limited. AA Driving School provides driving lessons, Road Code practice tests, licence test preparation, and the AA Defensive Driving Course. Official driver licence tests are conducted by NZTA-authorised testing agents. The New Zealand Automobile Association (Incorporated) separately acts as an agent of NZTA for the learner licence theory test.
