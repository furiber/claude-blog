What's in a one-hour lesson?

Each session with your qualified AA Driving Instructor is built around three parts and is adapted to your driving level. 

  1. Pre-drive briefing: your instructor sets clear goals for the session
  2. On-road coaching: progressive skill improvement through practice and repetition including steering, indicating, shoulder checks, and basic slow speed manoeuvres. Build up to congested urban areas, lane changing, roundabouts, higher speeds, motorway driving and night driving
  3. Debrief & next steps: honest feedback and what to practise before your next lesson

Common questions

AA Driving School professional driving lessons are highly recommended as they provide structured training tailored to your experience level, helping you build confidence, develop safer driving habits, and improve your chances of passing your driving test.

The number of lessons you need will depend on your driving experience, confidence and learning pace. Some learners need only a few lessons to prepare for a test, while others benefit from more structured coaching.

Our 5 Lesson Package offers great value and is a popular choice, giving you enough time to build key skills, gain confidence and receive personalised feedback from your instructor.

Watch the step by step video for a detailed explanation of how to book AA Driving School lessons.

To reschedule a driving lesson, you will need to cancel your existing lesson first. Once the lesson has been cancelled, the credit will be available in your account, which you can then use to rebook a lesson at your preferred date and time.

You can cancel a lesson by logging in to your AA Driving School profile and clicking the “X” next to the booking you wish to cancel. Once selected, confirm the cancellation to proceed.

If you have unused credits and would like to request a refund, please contact our team by calling 0800 223 748 or emailing ADriverTraining@aa.co.nz. Refund requests will be reviewed in accordance with our terms and conditions, and our team will advise you of the available options.
