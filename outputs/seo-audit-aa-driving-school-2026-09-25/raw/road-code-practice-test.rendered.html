AA Road Code Practice Tests are a valuable way to prepare for your theory test. They help you become familiar with the types of questions you may be asked, identify areas where you need further study, and build your confidence before sitting the real test.

AA Road Code Practice Tests are designed to closely reflect the format and style of the official learner licence theory test. The questions are based on the New Zealand Road Code and cover the same key topics tested in the learner licence exam, helping you become familiar with the types of questions you may encounter.
